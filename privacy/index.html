Privacy Policy — Haru Korean

Last updated: 2026-10-03

Haru Korean ("the app") is made by SDDT. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

Summary

  • No account, no sign-up, no own server. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.
  • We do not collect, sell or share personal data.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple / Google and the RevenueCat service (see below).

Data stored on your device

  • Your settings (meaning language, level, theme, reminder times) and your word lists (saved, learning, known).
  • On iPhone this data is shared with the app's own widgets and Apple Watch app through an App Group; on Android with the app's widgets. It is included in your normal device backups and deleted when you delete the app.
  • Pronunciation uses your device's built-in text-to-speech; text is not sent to us.
  • Reminders are scheduled locally by the operating system.

Purchases (RevenueCat)

If you buy Premium, the purchase is processed by the App Store or Google Play. To check whether Premium is active, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), which receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history for this app, device type and OS version, and IP address (for fraud prevention and regional pricing). RevenueCat privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Advertising

The current version shows no ads and contains no advertising SDK. If a future version adds ads for free users, this policy will be updated before release, and you will be asked for consent where the law requires it (for example in the EEA/UK, and App Tracking Transparency on iOS).

Analytics

The app contains no analytics or tracking SDKs. We see only the aggregate statistics Apple and Google provide to all developers (downloads, crashes if you opted in to share them).

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can delete all app data at any time by deleting the app. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on a server.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above.
